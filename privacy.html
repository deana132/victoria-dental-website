Privacy notice

The practice complies with the Data Protection Act and UK GDPR. This page explains what we hold, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Template for review. This notice reflects standard dental practice obligations and should be checked against the practice’s own procedures, ICO registration number and retention schedule before publication.

Who we are

Victoria Dental Practice, 2 Victoria Road North, Southsea, Hampshire PO5 1PX, is the data controller for the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us on 023 9281 1493 or at enquiries@victoriadentalpractice.net.

What we hold

  • Your name, address, date of birth and contact details
  • Your dental records: charts, treatment notes, x‑rays, photographs and clinical correspondence
  • Your medical history, including medication and allergies, which we ask you to confirm at each course of treatment
  • Details of appointments, payments and any plan membership
  • Any correspondence between you and the practice

Why we hold it

We process your information to provide dental care safely, to meet our legal and regulatory obligations as a dental practice, to manage appointments and payment, and to respond to enquiries. Health data is processed for the purposes of preventive and occupational medicine and the provision of health care and treatment, and is subject to a duty of confidentiality.

Who we share it with

We share information only where it is necessary: with specialists or laboratories involved in your treatment, with your dental plan provider where you are a member, with NHS bodies where treatment is provided under the NHS, and where we are required to do so by law. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Dental records are retained in line with current professional guidance, generally for at least eleven years after the last treatment, and for children until their twenty‑fifth birthday, whichever is longer.

Your rights

  • To be told what we hold and to receive a copy of it
  • To have inaccurate information corrected
  • To ask us to restrict how we use it, or to object to certain uses
  • To ask for erasure, where no legal or clinical reason requires us to keep it
  • To withdraw consent to any processing that relies on consent
  • To complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these, write to the practice at the address above.

This website

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The contact form composes an email in your own mail application rather than transmitting your details to a third party. Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap, and web fonts by Google Fonts; those services will receive your IP address as part of delivering the page.
